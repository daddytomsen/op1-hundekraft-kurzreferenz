Clouds

Granular textures, after Mutable Instruments Clouds.

A port of Mutable Instruments Clouds in granular mode: up to 24 grains over a buffer of about 2.7 seconds. Push an encoder to switch it to its page-2 value.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick clouds on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Position

Where in the buffer the grains come from

Push: Pitch, -24 to +24 semitones

[[cap:knob-g]]
Size

How long each grain is

Push: Dry/wet

[[cap:knob-w]]
Density

Below the middle the grains come regularly, above it at random

Push: Reverb

[[cap:knob-o]]
Texture

Grain shape. Above 75 % it adds diffusion

Push: Freeze on or off

Tip

Push orange to freeze whatever is in the buffer, then scrub it with blue. Freeze is not saved with the sound.

Note

Clouds' reverb runs at a quarter of the normal rate, so its tails are a little dark. In return the effect uses much less CPU.
