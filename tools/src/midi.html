MIDI control

Over USB MIDI, a computer or controller can press the OP-1's keys, turn its encoders, and set its knobs and the mixer directly. There are two groups of CCs, each on its own channel:

  • KEYS presses keys and turns encoders. It listens on the OP-1's MIDI channel unless you pick another one.
  • PARAM sets sound, effect, LFO and mixer values. It's off until you give it a channel.

Notes stay on the OP-1's own MIDI channel, and so do the MIDI LFO's CCs.

Pick the channels

  1. Hold [[Shift]] and press COM, then press [[T1]] for the OP-1 tab.
  2. Hold [[Shift]]. Below MIDI CH you now see KEYS CH, PARAM CH and FEEDBACK.
  3. Keep holding [[Shift]] and turn green for KEYS CH, white for PARAM CH and orange for FEEDBACK.

KEYS CH goes from OFF through OP (whatever the OP-1's channel is) to 1 to 16. PARAM CH is OFF or 1 to 16. Turn orange right to switch FEEDBACK on and left to switch it off. The OP-1 remembers your choices.

KEYS

  • Keys: a CC number presses a key (value 64 or more) or lets go (below 64). Examples: 5 help, 6 metronome, 7 to 10 synth, drum, tape and mixer, 11 to 14 T1 to T4, 38 REC, 39 PLAY, 40 STOP, 43 Shift, 49 COM. The keyboard keys can't be pressed this way.
  • Encoders: CC 102 to 105 turn blue to orange. Values 1 to 63 turn right, 65 to 127 turn left.

While an update sent over USB waits for [[T1]], MIDI presses no keys, so only you can confirm it.

PARAM

Values are absolute: 0 to 127 covers the knob's whole range. A change works like turning the knob, so it's saved in the sound.

A CC does nothing when its knob isn't there right now: the effect or LFO is off, the knob is a sample's start or end, the LFO is a quad LFO, or the engine has no second page. Put PARAM on the same channel as KEYS and the numbers in the table set values, while the other numbers still press keys.

Tip

Earlier builds had the mixer on the OP-1's channel, at CC 106 to 118. It's on the PARAM channel now, at the numbers above. Give PARAM a channel to use it.

Feedback

With FEEDBACK on, the OP-1 sends out what you do on it. Each key you press and each encoder you turn goes out as the same KEYS CC. When a PARAM value changes, by a knob, a new sound or a sequencer, its CC goes out on the PARAM channel. It doesn't send back what it just received, so a controller with motor faders or LED rings follows along without feedback loops.

MIDI sync: when the OP-1 syncs to MIDI, MIDI START restarts the pattern sequencer, Euclid and Dots so the first step lands on the master's first clock.

OP-Z controller mode is a hidden stock mode that can be switched on from a computer with SysEx. Most people won't need it.
