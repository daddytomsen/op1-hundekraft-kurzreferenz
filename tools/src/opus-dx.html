dx

dx, by LizardMan, plays DX7 voices: six FM operators and 32 algorithms. It reads voice banks, the .syx files DX7 libraries and editors use, and brings eight voices of its own: OPUS EP, OPUS BASS, OPUS BELL, OPUS BRASS, OPUS ORGAN, OPUS PAD, OPUS PLUCK and INIT VOICE. Its FM is built on Google's Music Synthesizer for Android (Apache License 2.0). The licence is in the workshop's licenses/opus.dx folder.

Your edits stay in the sound. Save it as usual and you get a user preset with the edited voice in it.

Loading banks. Connect your OP-1 to the workshop page and drop your .syx files on Add your own in the Presets section. A file holds one bank of 32 voices (4,104 bytes) or a single voice. If a file has several banks in a row, it's split into one bank each, named after the file with -1, -2 and so on, and each one counts towards the 16. The banks go in when the OP-1 restarts, so press [[T1]] when it asks. dx reads up to 16 banks. A bank's voices come after dx's own, and the screen shows the file's name as the bank. To remove one, tick it under What's on your OP-1 and press Delete selected. Copying .syx files over in disk mode doesn't work.
