Pew

Drawbar organ. Rotary speaker.

A drawbar organ in a jazz club, played through a rotary speaker. Each encoder is a whole organ control, so every position is a sound an organist would use: blue pulls out the drawbars, green adds the percussion, white adds chorus and then drive, and orange switches the rotary speaker. The screen moves with them: the drawbars slide out, notes fly up when you play, the smoke and the lamp follow white, and the red lines spin round the cabinet with the speaker.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick pew

[[cap:knob-b]]
Register

Drawbar settings from mellow to full: one flute bar, then the classic 888 bass, more and more upper bars, all bars out. They change in whole drawbar steps

Push: Pull the low bars (16', 5 1/3', 8') further out

[[cap:knob-g]]
Percussion

Off, then a soft second harmonic, then the sharp third with a fast decay, then more key click. It strikes only when no key was down, as on a Hammond

Push: Pull the middle bars (4', 2 2/3', 2') further out

[[cap:knob-w]]
Drive / chorus

Clean, then the scanner vibrato up to a full chorus, then tube drive up to a growl

Push: Pull the high bars (1 3/5', 1 1/3', 1') further out

[[cap:knob-o]]
Leslie

Three zones: brake (both rotors coast to a stop), slow and fast. The horn and the heavier drum take their time to speed up and slow down

Push blue, green or white to reach its page 2; push again to go back. Page 2 starts at nothing added, so a sound you haven't pushed plays as the register sets it. The settings are saved with the sound. Pew sounds saved before this version play differently, since the encoders mean other things now. The preset pack includes 16 Pew sounds.

Tip

Pew's lowest bar sounds an octave under the key. Keep the octave at 0 or above, or the bass turns to mud.
