Master LFO

Four LFOs that turn the master effect's knobs for you.

The master LFO moves the master effect (FX2) on its own, whichever synth or drum kit you play. It keeps running when you change sounds. Hold [[Shift]] and press [[mixer]] for the signal flow, then press [[T4]]. [[T1]] is the stock page and [[T2]] the FX2 page. On the master LFO page, [[T3]] takes you to the master effect itself, and [[T4]] from there brings you back.

There are four LFOs, one row each. Push blue, green, white or orange to pick LFO 1, 2, 3 or 4, then turn. Hold [[Shift]] and push an encoder to mute that LFO: it fades out, its row goes grey and MUTE shows where the amount was. Push again to bring it back. It keeps running while muted, so it comes back in time, and the mute is saved.

All amounts start at zero, so nothing moves until you turn one up. The LFO swings the knob around where you set it, and stays inside that knob's range. With + or - it only goes one way from your setting, but twice as far: set a knob to zero, pick + and amount 100, and the LFO opens it all the way. The knob itself doesn't move on screen, and a saved sound keeps the value you set. It works with every master effect, the stock ones and ours. The settings are kept after a restart.

Press [[T4]] again on this page to turn the whole master LFO off, and again to turn it back on, like [[T4]] on a synth's LFO. While it's off, the rows go dim and OFF shows where the amount was. It stays that way after a restart.
