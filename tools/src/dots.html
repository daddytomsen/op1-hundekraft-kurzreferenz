Dots

Six slots of up to 64 steps each, all playing at once at 1/16. Every step can have a note, a level, ratchets, a chance, a condition, a nudge and up to four effect locks.

Here is the whole loop. The keys it shares with Euclid work here too.

Make Dots play

  1. Hold [[Shift]] and turn white to pick a slot.
  2. Press [[REC]]. "REC" lights up top right.
  3. Play notes. Each key puts a step at the cursor and moves the cursor on. Turn blue to move the cursor yourself.
  4. Press [[REC]] again to stop recording. This step matters: while recording, orange sets the step level instead of starting the slot.
  5. Turn orange right. The slot starts on the next step. Turn it left to stop.

Recording controls

While [[REC]] is on:

ControlDoes
blueMove the cursor. [[Shift]] + turn erases while moving
greenSlot length, 1 to 64. [[Shift]] + turn right fills up to the next row of 16, copying the row above; left goes back a row
whiteParameter value. Push to switch between choosing the parameter and setting its value
orangeLevel of the step under the cursor, 1 to 127
Push orangeSwitch between step recording (REC) and live recording (LIVE)
Push blueOn an empty step: make a lock-only step. Push again to remove it

In LIVE, keys you play while the slot runs are recorded at the playhead, rounded to the nearest step. Hold a key longer for a longer gate.

Step parameters

Push white, turn it to choose a parameter, push again and turn to set it.

ParameterDoesRange
GATENote length5 % to 100 %, or TIE
RATCHRepeats within the step1 to 8
CHANCEProbability the step plays0 % to 100 %
CONDPlays only on loop a of bALL, 1:2, 2:2, 1:3 ... 4:4
NUDGEShift the step in time-12 to +12 ticks (of 96 per beat)
RAMPRatchet level rampOFF, UP, DN
FX1 to FX4Effect lock for the effect's blue to orange encoderOff, or 0 % to 100 %
SY1 to SY4Synth lock for the synth's blue to orange encoder (synth mode only)Off, or 0 % to 100 %

An effect lock moves that effect encoder to the stored value when the step plays, and back after the gate. A lock-only step (hollow square) changes the effect without playing a note: a quick way to throw a single delay or a filter sweep into a pattern.

A synth lock does the same for the slot's synth: the encoder jumps to the stored value when the step plays and goes back after the gate. Use it for a filter opening on one step, or a different wave on the last note of a bar. Drum mode has no synth locks, and the sampler's start and end aren't locked. If you save the sound while a lock holds, the saved sound keeps the locked value, as with effect locks. Patterns made before synth locks load unchanged.
