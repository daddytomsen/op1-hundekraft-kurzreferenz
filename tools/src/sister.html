SISTER

A cathedral reverb whose tail sings, rings in tune or swells backwards.

SISTER is Father's sister: a long stereo hall with three voices in its tail. Turn green toward the choir and the tail sings back what you played, on slowly drifting vowels (aah, ooh, eeh), like a choir far down the nave. At the other end it rings on organ pipes tuned to a root, its fifth and its octave, so drums and noise come back as a chord. White turns the tail around: each sound swells up backwards out of its own echo before it lands. Nothing switches modes. You can stop anywhere on a knob and it will sound like something.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick sister on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Space00–99 (about 2 s to 13 s)

A chapel to a cathedral: a longer, darker tail, more pre-delay, longer swells

[[cap:knob-g]]
VoiceChoir to pipes

The choir (vowels) to the pipes (in tune), with both together in the middle

[[cap:knob-w]]
SwellForward to reversed

The tail forwards to the tail reversed

[[cap:knob-o]]
MixDry to fully wet

Blend the dry sound and the hall. Near the top the hall gets a little brighter

Pipes key. Push green to set the key the pipes ring in. One turn step is one semitone, up to two octaves either way of C3. Hold [[Shift]] while turning for cents. The label shows the key, for example KEY C3. Push green again to go back. The key is saved with the sound.

The starting settings are about 60 for Space, 20 for Voice (the choir), Swell at 0 and 35 for Mix. Press [[T3]] to bypass or enable it. SISTER is mono on synth and drum, and stereo as a master effect.

The picture follows the sound. A veiled nun stands in a stone nave, and the nave gets deeper as Space grows. The petals of the rose window behind her head are singing mouths at the choir end and pipes at the other. They turn greener as you turn Voice and light up while the hall rings. The three candle flames grow with Swell and flicker with the tail. Mix is the red bar at the right.
