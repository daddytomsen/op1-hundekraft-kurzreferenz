Trigger guard

A worn key can bounce: one press reaches the OP-1 as press, release, press, and the note plays twice. The trigger guard catches that. It's on from the start and only watches the 24 note keys; the other keys work as before. The synth, the drums, the sequencers and USB MIDI out all get the keys after the guard. Its settings are on the TRIGGER GUARD page of the settings.

EncoderRowValues
blueTRIG. GUARDON or OFF
greenWINDOW MS10 to 300 in steps of 10, 50 at the start. A key pressed again this soon after you let go counts as a bounce
whiteWINDOWSPER NOTE times each key on its own. ALL shares one window between the keys, so a quick press on another key is dropped too
orangeHOLD RELEASEON holds each release back for the window, so a bounce can't cut the note short. OFF lets go at once

With HOLD RELEASE on, every note stops up to one window after you let go: 50 ms at the start. ALL also drops fast playing from one key to the next, so keep PER NOTE unless a key next to the worn one misfires.
