Sample editor

A proper editor for the sampler: trim, fade, normalise, reverse, set loop points and cut by transients. There is one for synth samples and one for sampled drum kits.

Open the editor

  • Synth: load the SAMPLER engine, then hold [[synth]] and press [[scissors]].
  • Drums: on a sampled drum kit, hold [[drum]] and press [[scissors]]. Play a pad to edit it.

The same combination closes the editor and saves. Tapping [[synth]] or [[drum]] by itself also closes it and enters that mode; holding the mode key for the scissors combination keeps working. Leaving for another mode closes it too.

Controls

ControlDoes
blue, greenStart and end markers. Keys play the selection straight away
Push blueSwitch blue and green to the loop start and end (synth only)
whiteZoom. [[Shift]] + turn moves the selection
orangeVolume of the whole sample, -60 dB to +24 dB, applied on close. [[Shift]] + turn scrolls
Push orangeSnap: zero crossings, transients, off
[[T1]]Trim to the selection. [[Shift]]: remove DC offset. Drums: fit (trim silence)
[[T2]]Zero the selection. [[Shift]]: fade
[[T3]]Normalise. [[Shift]] on drums: auto-cut
[[T4]]Reverse. [[Shift]] on drums: tune
[[Shift]] + [[left]]Undo a trim (synth), or the last auto-cut or tune (drums)
[[lift]], [[scissors]], [[drop]]Copy, cut, paste the selection, also to tape or another sampler

Fades: [[Shift]] + [[T2]] opens fade mode. [[T2]] picks in, out or both, [[T3]] picks linear, power or exponential, and blue sets the length. [[T1]] applies it, [[T4]] cancels.

Auto-cut (drums): [[Shift]] + [[T3]]. [[T2]] switches between hits, equal, time and tap mode. In hits mode it finds the first 24 hits, and blue sets the sensitivity from 1 to 7: higher finds quieter hits and hits closer together. In equal mode blue sets 1 to 24 slices. [[T1]] applies, [[T4]] cancels.

Lazy chop (drums): in auto-cut, press [[T2]] until it says TAP. Nothing plays yet: the screen says PRESS A KEY. The first key you press starts the sample from the beginning, and pad 1 starts at the very start. Tap any key where the next slices should begin: the first tap after that marks pad 2, the next pad 3, and so on up to 24. The keys don't play their pads while you chop. Each slice ends where the next one begins; the last one ends at [[STOP]], or at the end of the sound if you let it play out. With snap on ([[T3]]), a tap jumps to a hit within 30 ms of it. Taps are also moved about 4 ms earlier, to make up for the time the sound takes to reach you. [[T1]] keeps the slices, [[T4]] cancels, and [[Shift]] + [[left]] undoes them like any auto-cut. Once the sample has stopped, the next key starts over. The idea is BarneyBrown's.

Chop by time (drums): in auto-cut, press [[T2]] until it says TIME. The sample is cut into equal note values, starting at its first sound. blue picks the note value: 1/1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T or 1/32. green picks the tempo. All the way left it uses the OP-1's tempo; turn it right to say how many bars the sample is (1 to 8), and the grid fits the sample whatever tempo it was recorded at. The bars cover the whole sample, from its very start to the end of its last pad (or its last sound, if that comes later), so a loop that starts or ends on a rest keeps its grid. [[T3]] snaps each cut to the nearest zero crossing or hit within 30 ms, or leaves it on the grid. There are only 24 pads: if the grid has more slices, the top line says "24 of 64", pads 1 to 24 get the first 24 slices and the rest are left alone (grey lines). white moves which slice goes on pad 1, so you can chop a long loop in parts. [[T1]] applies, [[T4]] cancels, [[Shift]] + [[left]] undoes.

Tune (drums): [[Shift]] + [[T4]]. Each pad's pitch shows as a column, and the pad you played last is white. blue tunes that pad in semitones. green tunes all 24 pads together, so they stay the same distance apart. It stops when any pad reaches +48 or -48. [[T2]] sets every pad to the white pad's pitch, [[T3]] sets every pad back to 0. You hear the change as you turn. [[T1]] keeps it, [[T4]] puts the pitches back, [[Shift]] + [[left]] undoes after [[T1]]. Drum+ fine tune stays as it is on each pad.

Note

Synth samples can be up to 6 s, drum kits 12 s. Volume above 0 dB clips the loudest peaks.
