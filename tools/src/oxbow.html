Oxbow

An OB-style polysynth, after zvenson's OberVave.

Two oscillators with sync and cross-modulation into a state-variable filter, an internal filter envelope and a little vintage drift per voice. It comes from OberVave, zvenson's polysynth for the M-VAVE FM-1. Each encoder is a macro that moves several of these at once, so every position is a usable sound. Save and recall sounds with the normal OP-1 presets.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick oxbow

[[cap:knob-b]]
Tone

Opens the filter. The horns widen with it

Push: Resonance

[[cap:knob-g]]
Shape

The oscillators: pulse, saw + pulse, saw + saw, fifth, sync and cross-mod. The bell swings with it

Push: Filter type: LP 12, HP 12, BP 12, notch, LP 24, SEM

[[cap:knob-w]]
Contour

How much the filter moves with each note, and how fast. The breath grows with it

Push: Drive

[[cap:knob-o]]
Warmth

Detune and vintage drift between the voices. The birds walk out along the horns

Each corner belongs to one encoder: its name and value, the page-2 control under them, and an arc that fills as you turn. Shape's and Filter's arcs are notched, one notch per choice. Push blue, green or white to switch that encoder to its page 2: the line it edits lights up in its colour, the other dims, and the arc shows the page-2 value. Push again to go back. Settings are saved with the sound. The preset pack includes 16 Oxbow sounds.
