Windwood

A clarinet, built from the reed up.

Windwood is nvmsocool's clarinet. There are no recordings in it: a reed, the air flowing past it and the tube are simulated sample by sample, and the numbers were fitted to recordings of a real clarinet. Soft notes are close to a sine and slow to speak. Loud ones are bright and reedy.

A real clarinet jumps to a new register at written B4. Windwood doesn't, so the low tone carries on smoothly over the whole keyboard. MIDI notes 36 to 99 speak in tune; above that they go flat and thin.

The OP-1's envelope is the breath, so [[T2]] attack is the crescendo. About 135 ms matches the recordings, and a short attack gives a hard, punchy start. Every fresh note holds the reed shut for 46 ms before it speaks. Press a key while another is still sounding and the note is slurred: the air keeps moving and the pitch changes without a click.

Press [[synth]], hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]], then pick windwood in the list.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Breath

How hard you blow. The level follows only partly, so this is mostly tone. It starts at 80%

[[cap:knob-g]]
Growl

Centre is none. Turn down for a hummed growl, up for flutter-tongue at 22 to 32 Hz. Both ends run into chaos

[[cap:knob-w]]
Bore

How bright the tube is, half to twice the default

[[cap:knob-o]]
Air

Breath noise in the jet

The screen is a clarinet in sections. blue turns the dryer's dial and adds wind lines, green slides the saw (off centre it touches a section and shakes it), white lifts the microphone and orange lowers the vent. The key you hold opens a valve, and the wind comes on line by line as the envelope rises.

There is one page; encoder pushes and [[Shift]] add nothing.
