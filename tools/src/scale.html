Scale mode

Locks the keyboard to a scale, so wrong notes stop sounding.

  1. Press [[synth]], then [[T2]].
  2. Hold [[Shift]]. The scale view appears.
  3. Turn white to choose the key and orange to choose the scale. A message confirms it, for example "scale c major".
  4. Let go of [[Shift]] and play.

Each white key plays the note of its letter in the scale, black keys play only if they're in it. It works on everything that reaches the synth: the keyboard, MIDI in and the sequencers. The scales are major, minor, dorian, phrygian, lydian, mixolydian, locrian, harmonic minor, melodic minor, pentatonic major, pentatonic minor and blues. Turn orange to OFF to get the full keyboard back. Your setting is kept after a restart.

USB MIDI out sends the notes the keys play in the scale, so your DAW gets what the OP-1 plays. Notes from the OP-1's sequencers still go out as the sequencer plays them, before the scale.

While you hold [[Shift]] on the scale view, push orange to change how the keys map. A message shows the new mode:

  • Letter (the default): as above, each white key plays its letter in the scale.
  • Snap: a key outside the scale plays the scale note just below it.
  • White keys: the white keys play the scale's notes in order, with C as the root. Black keys are silent.
  • Mute: keys in the scale play as they are; the rest are silent.

Pentatonic and blues have fewer than seven notes, so Letter works like Mute with them. On a sampler+ slot, [[Shift]] on [[T2]] opens sampler+'s own page instead. Scale mode applies to every synth, so set it from a different synth slot.

blue and green keep their stock jobs on the scale page. The [[Shift]] shortcuts of the sequencers keep working with scale mode on. Out-of-scale chords can't be reached while it's on.
