Count-in

With the tape stopped, hold [[REC]] and press [[metronome]]. You get 4, 8 or 16 clicks, then the tape starts recording on the next beat. You can let go of [[REC]]. [[STOP]] cancels. Set the length on the settings page.

The metronome also accents beat 1 of every bar: an octave higher with TONE, louder and brighter with TICK and WOOD (Settings). Bars count from the start of the tape, or from where a count-in recording started.
