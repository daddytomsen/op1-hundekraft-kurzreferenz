matrix

matrix is an LFO type with up to eight rows. Each row is an LFO or an envelope with an amount and a target. Pick it on the LFO page with [[Shift]] + [[T4]]. It's LizardMan's, and ticked from the start. A sound saved with the earlier MATRIX opens in this one, but its rows may sound different.

Targets are the engine's knobs and their [[Shift]] layer, the envelope, the effect's knobs and their [[Shift]] layer, volume, pitch, a ring modulator, ducking, or the amount of another row. The drums have no volume or pitch target.

EncoderTurn[[Shift]] + turn
blueChoose a row, or the add placeRate, or note length in time
greenShape: sine, triangle, saw up, saw down, square, sample and hold, or envelopeHz or BPM
whiteAmountFree or restart on each key
orangeTargetBipolar or unipolar

On an envelope row, [[Shift]] and the four encoders set attack, decay, sustain and release. Push blue to mute or unmute a row, or to add one on the add place. [[Shift]] + push blue deletes the row. Push white to flip the amount. The envelope curve, linear, exponential or log, is MATRIX ENVELOPE CURVE on the OPUS PLUGINS settings page, and it counts for every matrix.
