drum+

drum+ is a second drum instrument on top of a sampled drum kit. It adds per-pad sound design: pitch envelope, retrigger, drive, choke groups, an envelope and a filter for every pad. Pads you haven't touched sound like stock.

Switch a kit to drum+

  1. Press [[drum]] and load a sampled drum kit.
  2. Hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]]. The drum type list opens.
  3. Turn blue to DRUM+ and press [[T1]]. The kit stays the same.

Load another kit or a DBOX to return to stock drums. A stock OP-1 loads a drum+ kit as a normal kit.

Note

When you open the type list again on a drum+ slot, it highlights DRUM, not DRUM+. Your slot is still drum+.

Page T1: play

Press [[T1]]. Play a pad to select it. Hold [[Shift]] for the second row.

Turning attack, decay or release switches the envelope on. Turning cutoff or resonance switches the filter to LP. Times go up to about 4.6 s.

Tip

Choke groups are how you make an open and closed hi-hat cut each other off. Put both pads in group 1.

Note

A fully loaded drum+ voice costs about three stock voices. The resource monitor shows the drum load while drums play.
