Key velocity

The OP-1's keys don't sense how hard you press them, but its accelerometer feels each key hit the case. With VELOCITY on, a harder hit plays a louder note. It starts off, and while it's off nothing changes. Only the keys use it: notes from MIDI and from the sequencers keep their own velocity.

To open its page, press [[Shift]] + [[help]], then [[T3]], then hold [[Shift]] and turn blue to KEY VELOCITY. Page 11 of the resource monitor shows each hit and each key's range, which helps when you set SENSITIVITY.
