Granular

Plays the slot's sample as grains.

A granular engine that plays a sampler recording as clouds of grains. C4 plays the recording at its original speed. Each grain is centred on the position and stays inside the recording.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick granular

The screen has one moving wave per knob in its colour. The recording runs along the top, with an arrow where the position is, and the live grains ride the blue wave.

To get sound into it:

  1. On a granular slot, press the input key (the microphone key) and record, just like on the sampler. The slot stays granular.
  2. Or: pick SAMPLER, record or load a sample, then switch the slot to GRANULAR. It reads the sampler's recording.
  3. Or: push white to browse the sampler presets, the same list as in sampler+. Turn white to choose and push it to load; push orange or press [[T1]] to cancel. The slot stays granular and plays the loaded sample.
[[cap:knob-b]]
PositionStart to end

Position in the recording

Push: Page 2: PITCH, the whole sound in semitones -24 to +24

[[cap:knob-g]]
Grain sizeAbout 10 ms to 370 ms

Push: Page 2: SHAPE, each grain's fade in and out hann, gauss, tukey, tri, pluck, swell

[[cap:knob-w]]
Spray0 to about 1.5 s

Random start jitter, and a little random pitch

Push: Sample browser: load a sampler preset

[[cap:knob-o]]
Density0.5x to 4x

How many grains overlap

Push: Page 2: sample gain 0x to 4x

Push the same encoder again to go back. While a page 2 is on, the screen shows its value: +7st, pluck or 1.0x. Hann is the soft shape Granular always had.

Note

Granular is silent until it has a recording. The granular presets in the pack are settings only.
