EVA

An astronaut's choir over the helmet radio.

A warm, sung pad with the sound of early-80s film scores. Two slightly detuned oscillators, a saw and a soft pulse, go through a lowpass with a gentle vowel colour on top, then through a wide ensemble chorus. Each voice has a slow vibrato and drifts a little on its own, so held chords slowly shift. The helmet radio sits on Com: low settings just warm the sound up, high settings bring in hiss, dropouts and the radio's beeps.

Push orange to set the beep's octave: the row reads OCT, and each click moves one step, an octave down, the original or an octave up (the ticks show which). Push again to return to Com. The setting is saved with the sound; the default is the original.

Note

With Com above 40 %, the radio sends its two tones: a high one when you start playing and a slightly lower one when you let go of the last key, each a quarter of a second, the second followed by a short burst of static. They don't follow the key you play. The end beep plays during the note's release, so a very short release on T2 cuts it off.
