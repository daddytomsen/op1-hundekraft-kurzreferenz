Sampler+

The synth sampler with envelopes, filtering and loop controls.

Choose sampler+ in the synth type browser ([[Shift]] + [[T1]]). A sampler slot keeps its recording; from another engine it becomes a sampler with whatever sample that slot holds. It stays a normal sampler sound, with sampler+'s settings saved alongside. Without sampler+ in your build the recording plays through the stock sampler; its extras come back when you install it again.

Set the sample region and loop points in the sample editor. Crossfade applies to forward loops. The white sample browser lists factory and user sampler presets: turn white to choose, push it to load while keeping sampler+, or push orange to cancel. [[T1]] also closes the browser.

Hold [[Shift]] on the T2 page to reach the play mode, portamento, cutoff and resonance. The filter starts off; turning cutoff or resonance switches it to low-pass, and pushing white without [[Shift]] picks another type. While you hold it, push white to step the envelope's pull on the cutoff and push orange to step velocity's pull on it. Both go from full down, through half down, off and half up, to full up, and the page shows them above those encoders.

Use T3 for effects and T4 for LFO modulation.
