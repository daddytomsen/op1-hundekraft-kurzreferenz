Plate

A plate reverb, after Maiko.

Plate diffuses the input into a reverb tail. Decay sets how long it rings; tone sets how bright it stays. The artwork is Maiko's, used with permission. Synth, drum and master each have their own reverb. On a synth or the drums it is mono; the master is stereo.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick plate on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Decay00–99

Short to long reverb tail

[[cap:knob-g]]
Tone00–99

Dark to bright

[[cap:knob-w]]
Pre00–99

Short to long delay before the reverb begins; rises more gently in the lower half

[[cap:knob-o]]
MixDry at 00, both at full level near 50, wet only at 99

Blend dry sound and reverb

The screen shows 00 to 99. It starts at about 50 for Decay, 60 for Tone, 33 for Pre and 20 for Mix. There is one page. Press [[T3]] to bypass or enable it.

New plate and CLASSIC

Since 3.3 Plate has two reverbs. Pick Plate and you get the new plate, with a decay of up to 20 seconds and a size you can set from a small room to a hall. The plate from before is still there as CLASSIC. Push white to switch between them. While CLASSIC is on, a small "classic" shows under Pre.

Sounds you saved with Plate before 3.3 load in CLASSIC and sound exactly as they did.
