Snowglobe

Shimmer reverb with a frozen heart.

A shimmer reverb whose tail climbs an octave at a time. At the top of orange the tail freezes into a pad that holds until you turn back.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick snowglobe on a synth or drum slot

To freeze a chord:

  1. Play and hold a chord with Snowglobe on, blue high.
  2. Turn orange all the way up. The screen frosts over.
  3. Let go of the keys. The frozen tail keeps ringing. Play over it.
  4. Turn orange back down to let it go.
Note

Snowglobe is heavier on the CPU than Washer. If you use it as master effect and on an instrument at the same time, each one runs its own copy.
