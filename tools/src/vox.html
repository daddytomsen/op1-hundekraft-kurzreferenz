VOX

Your voice, played on the keys.

Sing or talk into the OP-1, hold a chord, and VOX puts your words on those notes. The keys play a synth sound and your voice shapes it, so the chord you hold talks or sings. VOX takes your voice from the OP-1's input, like Amp: the mic, line, radio or USB. It splits your voice into up to 24 bands, and each band opens the same band of the synth. Chord mode, scale mode, the sequencers and MIDI all play the keys as usual.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick vox

Note

With the built-in mic, use headphones. Through the OP-1's speaker the mic picks up VOX's own output and it feeds back, so it only half works.

To use it:

  1. Tick VOX in the workshop's Synths list, then install it (a full firmware or Review changes).
  2. On the OP-1, press [[synth]], hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]]. Turn blue to vox and press [[T1]] to load it.
  3. Press the input key to switch monitoring on. Hold [[Shift]] and press the input key to choose mic, line, radio or USB.
  4. Hold a chord and talk or sing into that source.

The bars are the bands, and they move with the sound you hear. Each knob's setting is written under its name at the top. The green lines are your voice: the dim dashed one is how you sang it, and the solid one is where FORMANT moves it, with a small arrow pointing the way it went. The blue wave on the left changes with WAVE, red sparks grow with AIR and flash on "s" sounds, and the keys you hold show as blue dots with the chord's name. BANDS and HOLD from page 2 are always shown small at the bottom right, so you only need page 2 to change them.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Wave

The synth sound your voice shapes: saw, then square, then a thinning pulse, then a stack of three saws, then noise

Push: Bands: 8, 12, 16, 20 or 24. More bands are clearer, fewer sound more robotic. A new sound has 20

[[cap:knob-g]]
Formant

Moves your voice up or down the bands, up to 12 semitones each way. Down sounds bigger, up sounds smaller. The pitch stays the same

Push: Hold: Off or Hold

[[cap:knob-w]]
Response

Left is smooth and slow, right is sharp, fast and robotic

Push: back to the middle

[[cap:knob-o]]
Air

Lets the breath and the "s" and "t" sounds through, so words are easier to follow

Push: back to where a new sound starts

Push blue or green for page 2. Both knobs switch, so you can set Bands and Hold in one go; push either one again to go back. With Hold on, VOX keeps the last vowel you sang while you hold the keys, so you can sing a vowel, stop, and let the chord ring on with it. Let go of the keys and it lets go of the vowel. The settings save with the sound.

[[T2]] shows the input instead of the envelope: the source, the input gain, a level meter with a clip mark, and the note you're singing. Turn blue to set the gain, from -12 to +24 dB in 1 dB steps. Turn it up for a quiet mic, but keep the meter out of the clip mark. The gain belongs to the source, not the sound: mic, line, radio and USB each keep their own, and SNAP and Amp use the same one. Use [[T3]] to add an effect. The LFO on [[T4]] moves Wave.
