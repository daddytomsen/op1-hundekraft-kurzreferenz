MONOLIT

Three oscillators into a ladder filter, for bass and leads.

Every note plays three oscillators through a four-pole ladder filter. Each knob moves several settings at once, so every position gives you something usable. The amp envelope on T2, glide and the play modes work as on the other synths. Push blue, green or white for page 2.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick monolit

The screen is a monolith on a night horizon. The green ridge is the filter: narrow and steep when the cutoff is low, wide when it's open. Heat lifts the top and turns more of it red. The blue rings on the ground are the oscillators, two to seven of them as Shape goes up, and they ripple outward while the contour is open. The white dashed arc is the contour: a low, short arc for a pluck, a wide, high one for a swell.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Shape

One saw, then a saw with a square sub, two detuned saws, three saws, and at the top a wide stack

Push: Body: the interval of the third oscillator. SUB is an octave down, UNI is the same pitch, 5TH is a fifth, OCT is an octave up, SUB2 is two octaves down

[[cap:knob-g]]
Cutoff

From 80 Hz up, and the last quarter of the knob opens the filter all the way. It follows the keys, and it never closes below half an octave over the note you play

Push: Vel: how much your velocity opens the filter and changes the level. At 0 velocity only changes the level, a little

[[cap:knob-w]]
Contour

The filter envelope. Already at a fifth of the knob it sweeps the filter all the way, so the left half goes from a deep, snappy pluck to a longer one. The right half adds sustain, then a slow attack for swells

Push: Age: oscillator drift of 1 to 8 cents, and a small random cutoff offset on every note

[[cap:knob-o]]
Heat

The first half makes the sound fuller and more driven, as when a Minimoog's output is patched back into its own mixer. Towards the top the filter rings, and at the very top it whistles on its own

The page 2 values start at 0, which is a good place to be. The picture shows a sound with other values.

MONOLIT is heavy too: six voices use most of the CPU, so watch the CPU meter on big chords.

The preset pack has 12 MONOLIT sounds. Four are basses an octave down (ladder bass, pluck bass, sub drop and ladder ping). Then there is an acid line, three leads (fat lead, fifth lead and worn tape lead), a bright stab, a brass swell, a wide pad and a night drone.
