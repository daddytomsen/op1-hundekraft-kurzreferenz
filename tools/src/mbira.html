MBIRA

A thumb piano with bottle caps.

A kalimba: every note plucks a steel tine, rings in a wooden box and fades by itself. Holding the key doesn't stretch the ring, but letting go starts the release on T2, so a short release cuts it. Turn up the buzz and bottle caps rattle while the tine swings.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick mbira

[[cap:knob-b]]
Strike

Soft thumb, round and dark, to a hard nail with a bright click

Push: Ring: how long notes ring, 1.6 to 8 seconds

[[cap:knob-g]]
Tine

The tine's material: wood, steel in the middle, glass

Push: Double: a second, slightly sharp tine that beats against the first

[[cap:knob-w]]
Box

The wooden box's resonance and thump. Off at zero

Push: Wah: a band-pass sweeping at about 5 Hz

[[cap:knob-o]]
Buzz

Bottle caps rattling while the tine swings wide

Push blue, green or white to reach its page-2 setting, push again to go back. The labels on screen change to RING, DOUBLE and WAH. Page-2 settings are saved with the sound.
