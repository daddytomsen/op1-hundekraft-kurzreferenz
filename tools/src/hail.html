HAIL

Clouds of grains, after Mutable Instruments Beads.

HAIL records what comes in and plays many short, overlapping grains from it: pitched, reversed, smeared and sent into a reverb. It's based on the granular mode of Mutable Instruments Beads, with a buffer of about 1.5 seconds and up to 12 grains at once (8 while the reverb is on).

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick hail on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Time

Where new grains start in the recording, from just now to 1.5 seconds back

Push: Pitch: -24 to +24 semitones, one per click; Shift + turn for cents

[[cap:knob-g]]
Size

Grain length up to 4 seconds. Below a third the grains play backwards

Push: Mix: dry to wet

[[cap:knob-w]]
Shape

Grain envelope: square, decay, bell, reverse ramp

Push: Space: reverb in the first half, feedback added in the second

[[cap:knob-o]]
Density

Left half: regular grains, faster to the left. Right half: random grains, more to the right. The middle starts none

Push: freeze

Push blue, green or white to switch that row to Pitch, Mix or Space; push it again to go back. The dial and meter follow the new control and the label changes. Pitch steps one semitone per click, so fifths and octaves are exact; hold [[Shift]] while turning for cents. Space is reverb in its first half, then feedback.

Push orange to freeze: nothing new is recorded and the grains keep playing what's in the buffer. The orange row shows FROZEN; turn still changes Density. Push again to unfreeze. Freeze isn't saved with the sound.

Tip

Start with Density a little left of the middle and Size around 60 %. Push green to select Mix, then turn it fully up to hear only the grains.
