Trail

A melody that slowly wanders off.

Hold a note and Trail plays a short phrase in your scale. Each phrase is built from a short idea of four or eight steps that comes back, changed a little, and the phrase ends on the root or a chord tone. Leave it running and Trail keeps working on that idea: it moves a bit of the tune up a step, turns it upside down or plays it backwards, so the phrase drifts somewhere new while you play over it.

Select Trail in the sequencer browser and enable [[seq]]. Hold a note to play its phrase; release the last key to stop. Hold Shift when releasing a key to latch playback. Playing and releasing a key without Shift stops the latch. [[STOP]] stops playback too. A latched part keeps playing when you come back from the other mode: it pauses while synth or drum mode is shown and carries on when you return.

Trail starts with a 16-step minor phrase. C4 plays it at the selected root; other keys transpose it by their distance from C4. It repeats exactly with Evolution at zero. Raise Evolution and Trail develops one part of the phrase at the end of a loop.

Character runs from calm to restless. Calm phrases move in small steps, put their notes on the beat, repeat their idea and land firmly on the root. Restless ones leap further, push notes onto the off-beats, slip in neighbour notes before the beat and repeat less. Character also picks how the phrase develops: calm phrases mostly move a part up or down a step, restless ones flip it or shift it by a step.

Push blue to switch PLAY/TUNE. Push green for a new phrase and white for new notes on the same rhythm. Push orange to undo the last change; push it again to bring the change back. Several turns of one knob in a row undo as one change. A push that has nothing to do right now is dimmed. Character, Scale and Range rebuild the phrase from the same start; Density changes which steps sound.

FOLLOW uses global Scale when it's on, otherwise Chord's scale; if neither is on, Trail keeps its own. Switch back to LOCAL to edit Trail's own root and scale. Undo also works on swing and gate. Lock and Answer stay as they are when you undo.

Answer turns Trail into a call and response partner. Your keys play as usual and Trail listens. Stop playing for about a beat and it answers on its own rhythm grid: your phrase with a new last note, the same phrase a step higher or lower, or its shape turned upside down. Calm leans toward the first, restless toward the last. Play again whenever you like; the answer stops as soon as you do. Trail remembers up to 16 notes of each call. While Answer is on, Density, Evolution, Range and Steps and the pushes that change the phrase are dimmed, because the answer doesn't use them.

Available scales are Major, Minor, Dorian, Mixolydian, Lydian, Major pentatonic, Minor pentatonic and Chromatic. The white line shows the phrase's pitches, with a dot for each step and a hollow dot for a rest. The red dot is the step playing now. The two grey lines below are the phrase as it was before its last two changes, so you can watch it develop. Settings, Answer and the current phrase are saved and come back after a restart, with playback stopped.
