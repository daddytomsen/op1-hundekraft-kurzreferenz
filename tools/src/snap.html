SNAP

Pitch correction, from polite to obvious.

SNAP pulls your singing onto the notes. It takes your voice from the OP-1's input, like Amp, and moves each note you sing to the nearest note of the key. Set gently, it nudges a shaky note into tune so nobody notices. Set hard, every note snaps to the key, the sound everyone knows from the radio. Hold keys and each one adds a copy of your voice at that key's pitch, with the key's envelope, so one chord gives you a small choir of yourself. A tuner needle shows how far off you sang.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick snap

Note

With the built-in mic, use headphones. Through the OP-1's speaker the mic hears SNAP's own output instead of your voice, and SNAP can't find your pitch.

To use it:

  1. Tick SNAP in the workshop's Synths list, then install it (a full firmware or Review changes).
  2. On the OP-1, press [[synth]], hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]]. Turn blue to snap and press [[T1]] to load it.
  3. Press the input key to switch monitoring on. Hold [[Shift]] and press the input key to choose mic, line, radio or USB.
  4. Sing into that source. No key needs to be held.

Your voice comes out about 13 ms late, the time SNAP needs to hear a note's pitch.

The lines are the notes. The thin white line is the pitch you sing and the thick green one is where SNAP puts it, so you can see it step from note to note. Each key you hold adds a blue dashed line at its pitch: that's your choir. On the left is the note SNAP is pulling you to, with a needle under it like a tuner's. It leans left when you sing flat and right when you're sharp, and points straight up when you hit the note. Each knob's setting is written under its name at the top (VOICES as a small slider from ME to KEYS), and TARGET and RANGE from page 2 are always shown at the bottom.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Speed

How fast your pitch moves to the note. All the way left it jumps from note to note, the robotic sound from pop records. Turn right for a slower glide that sounds more natural

Push: Target: Auto, Chrom or Keys

[[cap:knob-g]]
Human

Lets long notes and almost-right notes move more freely, so vibrato and small slides stay in

Push: Range: Low, Mid or High

[[cap:knob-w]]
Formant

Makes your throat sound bigger (left) or smaller (right) without changing the pitch

Push: back to 0, your own voice

[[cap:knob-o]]
Voices

Left is your corrected voice alone. The middle adds the voices from the keys you hold. Right is the key voices alone

Push: back to the middle

Push blue or green for page 2. Both knobs switch, so you can set Target and Range in one go; push either one again to go back.

  • Target. Auto uses the key from scale mode or chord mode when one is on, otherwise all 12 notes. Chrom is all 12 notes. Keys sings the last key you pressed and stays on it after you let go, so you can play the melody on the keys.
  • Range. Pick the one that fits your voice. Low goes down to 70 Hz for deep voices, Mid (the default) to 100 Hz, and High to 150 Hz. The higher the range, the shorter the delay: Low 16 ms, Mid 13 ms, High 8 ms.

[[T2]] shows the input instead of the envelope: the source, the input gain, a level meter with a clip mark, the note SNAP hears and the delay. Turn blue to set the gain, from -12 to +24 dB. Like VOX's, it belongs to the source and not the sound. Use [[T3]] to add an effect. The LFO on [[T4]] doesn't move SNAP's knobs. The settings save with the sound.

There are twelve SNAP presets: hard snap, natural, choir of one, keys only, duet, slow glide, big throat, small throat, bright singer, choir pad, low singer and voice lead.
