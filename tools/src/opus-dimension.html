dimension

dimension is a six-voice synth by LizardMan. One oscillator morphs from noise through pulse, square and saw to saw with a sub. It runs into a four-pole low-pass filter and a slow ensemble that thickens the sound.

EncoderTurn
blueWaveform: noise, pulse, square, saw, saw with sub
greenUnison: the ensemble's depth
whiteFilter frequency, 30 Hz to 18 kHz
orangeResonance

[[T2]] is the amp envelope as usual. A big chord doesn't come out louder than a single note, because chords are compressed together.
