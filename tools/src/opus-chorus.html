chorus

chorus, by LizardMan, has one to three voices and a triangle or sine sweep, and it's stereo as the master effect. On screen each voice is a stack of waves in its encoder's colour.

EncoderTurn[[Shift]] + turn
blueRate, 0.05 to 8 HzVoices, 1 to 3
greenDepthFeedback
whiteDelayLow cut with the sine sweep; with the triangle, delay in finer steps
orangeMixHigh cut with the sine sweep; with the triangle, mix in finer steps

The sweep, triangle or sine, is CHORUS SWEEP on the OPUS PLUGINS settings page. It's one choice for every chorus, not saved with a sound.
