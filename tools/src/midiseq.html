MIDI sequencer

A sequencer for notes and MIDI CCs on four tracks that play along with the tape. Start recording with the first note or a count-in, then quantize the timing if needed. Each track takes notes from the keyboard, from the OP-1's sequencers or from a USB MIDI channel, and plays them to the synth, into a sequencer or out over USB MIDI.

Switch it on

The MIDI sequencer is off until you switch it on. While it's off, SEQ and TAPE work as usual.

  1. Hold [[Shift]] and press [[help]], then press [[T3]]. The settings page opens.
  2. Turn green right: MIDI SEQ shows ON. Turn it left to switch off.

The setting is kept after a restart.

Open the sequencer

Hold [[seq]] and press [[tape]]. The sequencer replaces the tape screen. Press [[tape]] again to go back to the tape, or another mode key to go to that mode. The tracks keep playing with the tape while the view is closed.

On top you see the selected track, the position in bars, beats and sixteenths, the time, and the track's input and output, for example I → M1.

Inputs and outputs

SettingAs input, recordsAs output, plays
INotes you play on the keyboardNotes to the synth; CCs to its knobs (see below)
SNotes the OP-1's sequencers playNotes into the stock sequencer, as key presses
M1 to M16Notes and CCs on that USB MIDI channelNotes and CCs out over USB MIDI on that channel

Inputs I and S also record CCs that come in on the OP-1's own MIDI channel.

Record a track

  1. Pick a track with [[T1]] to [[T4]]. Push white and turn it to pick the input.
  2. From stop, hold [[REC]] or press [[Shift]] + [[REC]] to arm. The first note on the chosen input starts playback and MIDI recording. You can release REC after the first note; [[STOP]] ends it. A note from another input cannot start it.
  3. For a count-in, hold [[REC]] and press [[metronome]]. You can release REC during the clicks. After the count, PLAY starts and recording keeps going until you press STOP. The count-in is the same one described under Count-in. While the tape already plays, held REC records while held, or Shift + REC toggles latched recording.
  4. The playhead turns red while it records. New notes are added to what's there: nothing is erased.

The quantize grid is saved on the OP-1. A grid label such as Q16 appears beside the position. When you finish a take, up to its first 256 notes snap to the grid. To quantize later, press [[Shift]] + push blue; that handles up to 256 notes in the selected range. Note lengths and CC timing stay as they are. Finish recording before quantizing a selection. Leaving the view cancels a pending armed start or count-in.

Note

While the sequencer view is open, REC belongs to the sequencer, so the tape doesn't record audio. The one exception is Print, which records audio. Close the view with [[tape]] to record audio again. The song is one file on the OP-1, shared by all four tapes.

Print a track onto the tape

Hold [[Shift]] and press [[drop]] to record the selected track onto the tape's selected track. The tape starts one beat before the first note and records until one bar after the last note ends. Only that track plays. With a selection, only its notes are printed.

The recording adds to any audio already on the track, and undo takes it back in one step. When it's done, the MIDI track is muted, so you don't hear it twice. Press [[STOP]] to end a print early.

Print needs the tape stopped and the track's output set to I. With the loop on, the first note has to be inside the loop, and the print ends at the loop end. The idea is u/Infamous_Call_6492's.
