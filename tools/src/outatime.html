outatime

A time-travel synth that drifts between 1955 and 2015.

Every voice plays three oscillators, the flux capacitor's three arms. Year picks their wave and Speed lets them drift through the years around it. Power is a strong overdrive, and Trail is the ignition: each note opens a resonant filter that then burns down. The amp envelope on T2, glide and the play modes work as on the other synths. Push blue, green or white for page 2.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick outatime

The screen is the time machine. The time circuits show years only: DEST is the year you set, PRES is where the first arm has drifted to, and LAST is the year you had before the last change. While you play, the car speeds up, and when the needle reaches your speed the fire trail starts. The flux capacitor's rods glow brighter with more power and breathe with the drift, and current runs along the cables.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Year

1955 to 2015. A round triangle in 1955, like an organ, a saw by 1985, and toward 2015 a sine folded up to 8 times, which sounds like glass. The filter opens as the years go by

Push: Spread: detunes the two outer oscillators, up to 20 cents

[[cap:knob-g]]
Speed

0 to 88 mph: the time drift. The three oscillators circle the year a third of a cycle apart. At 88 mph they swing up to 30 years either way, almost twice a second

Push: Tone: darker or brighter within the year

[[cap:knob-w]]
Power

0 to 1.21 GW of overdrive, up to 40 times. The level is matched, so more power doesn't make it louder. Each note is driven on its own, so chords stay clear

Push: Flux: an ensemble like the old string machines, moving the pitch up to 22 cents

[[cap:knob-o]]
Trail

The ignition. Each note opens the filter up to three octaves and it burns down in 40 ms to 1.4 s. Turning it up also adds resonance

Hold Shift and turn orange to pick the drive: warm, hard, tube or fold. The trail tab shows which one while you hold Shift. Your choice is saved with the sound. A new sound starts warm, and so does every sound you saved before. Older firmware plays all outatime sounds warm.

Chords drive and limit together, as one signal, so a chord breaks up the way a single loud note does.

Page 2 starts at 0 on a new sound: no spread, no flux and a darker tone. For the starting sound of LizardMan245's original, load the outatime preset.

outatime is heavy. Six voices high on the keyboard use about 83% of the CPU, so watch the CPU meter on big chords.

There are twelve outatime presets: outatime (the original's own sound, 1985 at 18 mph), 1955 organ, 2015 glass, 88 mph, clip lead, drift wash, flux bass, fold bell, gigawatt, hover pad, scorch and tine keys.

outatime is by LizardMan245. Kennel port by hundekraft.
