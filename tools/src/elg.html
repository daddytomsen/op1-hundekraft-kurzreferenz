Elg

A moose calling under the northern lights.

Elg is Norwegian for moose, and every note is a moose call. A low, growling voice opens its throat from "oo" towards a wider vowel while the pitch scoops up and sags. A chest resonance, a breath of air and a sub sit under it. Each voice calls again on its own every few seconds, so a held chord turns into a herd calling across a frozen lake. orange brings in a slow aurora pad behind the animal: three saws whose two bright bands drift like curtains of light.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick elg

On the screen the antlers spread with blue and toss on each call. The breath clouds grow with green and puff when a call starts. The moon darkens as white goes down and glows as it goes up, and the aurora ripples with orange.

Classic

Push white to switch to CLASSIC, the Elg from before: three saws per voice through a growl drive and a slow-moving filter. A small "classic" shows at the lower left. Push white again to go back. Sounds you saved with the old Elg, and the Elg presets, open in CLASSIC and sound as they did. A new Elg sound starts in the new mode.

In CLASSIC the encoders work as before:

[[cap:knob-b]]
IntervalUnison, fifth, octave, octave + fifth, two octaves

Interval of the third saw. One step a click, in order, then round again. Each new interval fades in

[[cap:knob-g]]
Detune0 to about 20 cents

Detune between the saws. Slow beating to wide chorus

[[cap:knob-w]]
Timbre40 Hz to 6 kHz, 1x to 4x drive

Opens the filter and adds growl at the same time

[[cap:knob-o]]
MovementStill, then 0.03 Hz to 2 Hz

LFO speed and depth on filter, level and pitch drift
