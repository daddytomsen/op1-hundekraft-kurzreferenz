Master effect routing

Put the master effect on what you play, or on sends from the tape.

Normally the master effect (FX2) processes the whole mix. You can point it at something else. Hold [[Shift]] and press [[mixer]] for the signal flow, then press [[T2]] for the FX2 page. Press [[T1]] to go back.

EncoderNameDoes
blueFX2 sourceMASTER: the whole mix, as stock. INSTRUMENT: only the synth or drums you're playing live; the tape stays dry. AUX: the sends from the tape tracks
greenFX2 outputOnly with INSTRUMENT or AUX. STEREO MASTER: the effect is heard on the master but not recorded. MONO TAPE: the effect is recorded in mono onto the tape track you record to

With AUX, the mixer's [[T1]] page gets a small send knob under each track's pan. Hold [[Shift]] and turn a track's encoder to set its send, from 0 to 100. Push that encoder to make [[Shift]] + turn set the pan again; push it once more to go back to the send. When you record with MONO TAPE, the send of the track you record to is muted, so it can't feed back into itself. The routing and the sends are kept after a restart.

Only what a track sends reaches FX2 on AUX: a track at send 0 stays dry, and so does the synth or drums you play live.

FX2 routing works with every master effect: the stock ones such as spring, delay and CWO, the filter, and the workshop's plugin effects.
