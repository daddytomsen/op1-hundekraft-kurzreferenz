SID

An 8580-inspired chip synth.

Based on Hermit’s cRSID, SID combines three oscillators per note with a resonant filter. The modes cover triangle, saw, pulse, noise, oscillator sync and ring modulation. It is inspired by the 8580 chip, not an exact copy of one. Use the OP-1's normal ADSR page to shape each note. Turn blue to choose a waveform and white to open or close the filter. The cutoff number shows control position, not hertz.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick sid

[[cap:knob-b]]
Mode

Triangle, saw, pulse, noise, sync or ring

[[cap:knob-g]]
Voices / width / interval

In triangle, saw and noise: blend the octave and fifth. In pulse: width, about 3–97%. In sync and ring: the interval between source and carrier, +0 to +24 semitones (up to 4 times)

[[cap:knob-w]]
Cutoff

Open the filter, 00–99

[[cap:knob-o]]
Resonance

Emphasize the cutoff, 00–99

SID has one control page; pushing the encoders or holding [[Shift]] adds nothing.
