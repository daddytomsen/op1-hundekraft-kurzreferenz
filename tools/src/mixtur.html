Mixtur

Undertones below every key.

A subharmonic organ after Oskar Sala's Mixtur-Trautonium. Four dividers play undertones of the key: the key is the top note, and the dividers add notes at 1/n of its frequency below it. Two formant filters turn the result into vowels. It sounds dark, vocal and slightly unearthly. Each divider offers OFF and 1 to 24. The starting values are 1, 2, 3 and 5.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick mixtur

[[cap:knob-b]]
Divider A

Undertone 1 to 24, or off

Push: Formant 1, 180 Hz to 4 kHz

[[cap:knob-g]]
Divider B

Push: Formant 2, 500 Hz to 5.7 kHz

[[cap:knob-w]]
Divider C

Push: Resonance, smooth to ringing

[[cap:knob-o]]
Divider D

The default 1, 2, 3, 5 gives a minor chord under the key. Set the four dividers to different numbers: two dividers on the same number are muted. All four off is silence. Very low undertones fade out below about 40 Hz, so the bottom keys play fewer of them.

Mixtur is one of the six Musica Obscura modules, ultralazr's set of strange instruments from music history: two synths (Mixtur and Arc), two effects (Palme and In a room) and two sequencers (Oracle and Peal). The synths and effects have alternate encoder settings where their tables show them: push that encoder to switch and push again to return. The sequencers use the [[Shift]] controls described in their own sections. The author's README has the full story behind each one, and sound demos.
