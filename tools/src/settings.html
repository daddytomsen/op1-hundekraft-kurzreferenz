Settings

[[Shift]] + [[help]], then [[T3]]. The page has four rows, one per encoder, with the encoder's colour beside each. The first two pages are SETTINGS; plugins that have settings follow them, one or two pages each, in alphabetical order. The dots at the top right show which page you're on.

Hold [[Shift]] and turn blue to change page. While [[Shift]] is held, the rows dim and the title turns blue between two arrows.

orangeCLICK: the metronome's sound. TONE is the OP-1's own beep, TICK a short tick with no pitch, WOOD a dry woodblock knockThe metronome through the master effect, or not (the default)

The second page has three rows: blue is USB AUDIO and green is TRACKS FROM (see USB output). white is PICKER STEP. orange does nothing there.

PICKER STEP sets how many encoder clicks move the lists one row: 1, 2 or 4. It works in the synth, effect, LFO and sequencer lists and in the synth and drum preset browsers. 4 is the stock feel. The idea is u/Infamous_Call_6492's.

Opus plugin settings

Plugin files made for Opus that have settings share one page, OPUS PLUGINS, after all the other settings pages. It only shows when one of them is on the OP-1. Each row has the plugin's name in grey, the setting and its value.

Turn blue to pick a row and orange to change it. Push orange to put it back to the plugin's default. A change works straight away. These settings belong to the plugin, so they count for every sound that uses it. With the bundled files the page holds CHORUS SWEEP, MATRIX ENVELOPE CURVE, PLATE COPIES and WAVE AMPLITUDE (the art of plate (Opus)'s tail) and SIM SHOW VALUES. A file you add yourself brings its own rows, and the list scrolls when there are more than six.

TICK and WOOD won't clash with the key of what's on the tape. The metronome skips the master effect, so a reverb or delay on the master doesn't smear the click. Push orange to send it through the master effect anyway: FX then shows after the sound's name. Push again to take it out.

On a plugin's page, a turn changes the row: on/off rows turn on to the right and off to the left, other rows step through their values. A push turns an on/off row on or off and sets any other row back to its default. Everything is saved on the OP-1.
