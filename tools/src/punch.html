Punch-in

Punch-in uses tape markers. Hold [[tape]] and press [[REC]] to arm it: the markers turn red. Play the tape. Recording starts at the next marker and stops at the one after. Press [[STOP]] to stop, or [[tape]] + [[REC]] again to disarm.

Quantized punch

Normally a take starts the moment you press [[REC]]. With quantized punch it waits for the next step. On the PUNCH page of the settings:

RowValues
QUANT PUNCHON or OFF. It starts off
PUNCH GRIDBAR, 1/4, 1/8 or 1/16

Press [[REC]] and recording starts at the next step. When you stop, it runs on to the next step. Press [[STOP]] twice to stop at once. Bars count from the start of the tape. With the loop on, the loop end counts as a step. Marker punches snap to the grid too. Count-in recordings start on their count, as always. The idea is u/Infamous_Call_6492's.
