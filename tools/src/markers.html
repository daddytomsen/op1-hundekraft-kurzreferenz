Markers

On the tape screen:

ControlDoes
Push orangeSet a marker at the playhead
[[Shift]] + push orangeRemove the marker under the playhead
[[Shift]] + turn orangeJump to the next or previous marker, also while playing
Hold [[Shift]] + [[left]] or [[right]]The jump to the next grid line repeats: first after about half a second, then about seven a second

Up to 32 markers, saved with the tape. Plain orange still sets the record level. The repeat doesn't work while you record, or with STEFAN on. The idea is u/Infamous_Call_6492's.
