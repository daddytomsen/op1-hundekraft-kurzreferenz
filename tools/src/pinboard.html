PINBOARD

A synth you patch with pins.

PINBOARD is a synth built around a pin board, inspired by the EMS VCS3: its pin matrix, its diode-ladder filter, its ring modulator and its trapezoid envelope. It has two oscillators, a noise source, a ring modulator and a filter that squeals when you push it. A third oscillator runs slow or fast and is there to modulate the others. The board is a grid of eight sources and eight destinations. Put a pin where a source crosses a destination and that source moves it: osc 3 wobbles the filter, the envelope sweeps the pitch, the tilt of the OP-1 bends a note. Each voice has its own filter, so chords stay clean.

Press [[synth]], hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]], then pick PINBOARD in the list.

Osc 2 tune picks unison, detune, fifth, octave, octave down, fourth, twelfth or two octaves. Push orange to edit the board; the other pushes do nothing.

The pin board

The rows are the sources: osc 3, the envelope, tilt X, tilt Y, noise, osc 2, the key you play and a random value for each note. The columns are the destinations: osc 1 pitch, osc 2 pitch, filter frequency, response, shape, ring, noise level and level. A pin comes in three kinds. A solid white pin is a full connection, a green ring is a gentle one, and a red pin with a bar is full and inverted.

Turning orange steps through 64 ready-made patterns, grouped by what they do: pitch (direct, vibrato, chorus, siren, dive), level and shape, filter, tilt, ring and FM, noise, random, drums, gentle and chaos. Direct has no pins at all. After the last pattern comes CUSTOM, the board of your own.

Edit the board

Push orange and the screen turns into the big board. Turn blue to pick the source, green to pick the destination, and white to set the pin where they cross: off, full, gentle or inverted. You hear each change at once. Push orange again to go back. A T key, the [[T2]] page or another sound also takes you out.

If PATCH is on a pattern when you push, that pattern is copied into CUSTOM first, replacing what was there, and PATCH moves to CUSTOM. So the quick way to make your own board is to find a pattern that's close and push.

CUSTOM is saved with the sound. Turning PATCH never changes it, so you can try a few patterns and turn back to CUSTOM. A sound that never had a board of its own starts with an empty one.

Tilt is the OP-1's own tilt sensor, smoothed over about 30 ms.

The envelope

PINBOARD has its own envelope, a trapezoid, and it is the voice's level. Press [[T2]] to set it. blue is attack, from 2 ms to 2 s. green is the time it stays on top, up to 4 s. white is decay, from 3 ms to 15 s. orange is the pause before it starts again, from 0.5 ms to 5 s.

While you hold a key the trapezoid repeats, and when you let go it finishes its decay. Turn orange all the way up and it reads MANUAL: no repeat, it holds on top while the key is down. Key velocity and the OP-1's normal envelope don't reach this voice.

The pins, the scope, the filter and the tilt dial show the sound as you turn. With [[Shift]] held, the main parts go dark and the [[Shift]] parts light up: osc 2's wave, the noise speckles, the ring and the orbit of osc 3.

The presets are dark drone, ring bells, whistler, wind, siren, zap, trapezoid drum, growl bass, looping pulse, ring tremor, space wah, random bleeps and chaos.
