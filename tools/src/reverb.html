Reverb

Reverb, after Mutable Instruments Elements.

Based on the Elements reverb from Mutable Instruments, drawn as Kali with art by DAN76. Her arms count the decay, the flames show the tone. The skulls and flames change brightness continuously.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick reverb on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Time

How long the tail rings, up to almost endless

[[cap:knob-g]]
Tone

Dark to bright

[[cap:knob-w]]
Diffusion

How smeared and dense the reflections are

[[cap:knob-o]]
Mix

Dry to wet

Note

Reverb runs at half rate, so its tail is a little dark. In return it leaves more CPU for the rest of the sound.
