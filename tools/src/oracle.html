Oracle

Oracle and Peal are sequencers from Musica Obscura. Pick either with [[Shift]] + [[seq]].

Oracle turns four geomantic figures, the Mothers, into a 16-step pattern with four lanes. Each lane plays an interval above the key in synth mode, or its own pad in drum mode. Hold a key to play; release it to stop at the next step. Hold [[Shift]] while releasing the last key to latch the pattern. Hold [[Shift]] and turn orange left to stop it.

[[Shift]] + push white switches lane editing on or off. While EDIT shows, a key sets the selected lane's interval above the current root, or its drum pad, instead of starting the pattern. [[Shift]] + push orange cycles ten chord voicings in synth mode.

[[lift]] copies the pattern and [[drop]] pastes it. [[scissors]] silences the selected lane; [[Shift]] + [[scissors]] resets the whole pattern. [[Shift]] + the first four white keys picks pattern 1 to 4. [[Shift]] + G, A, B and C in the high half mutes Fire, Air, Water and Earth; [[Shift]] + the high D# mutes all. [[Shift]] + the high C# starts or stops the pattern; hold [[REC]] too to record it to tape from the start. Settings are saved; playback comes back stopped.
