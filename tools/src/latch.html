Latch

Notes keep sounding after you let go of the keys.

Latch is for drones. On the synth's envelope page ([[T2]]), push orange to turn it on or off. To choose between LATCH and HOLD, hold [[Shift]] on the same page and push green. The choice shows in green at the bottom of that page, above MODE, and the OP-1 remembers it. While latch is on, a slim keyboard strip shows at the top of the synth screens, left of centre: 24 thin lines, one per key on the OP-1 keyboard, with the black keys shorter. Free keys are dim blue and latched keys are pink. Next to it, LATCH or HOLD is followed by the number of notes held. A note stays on its own key in the strip even if you change octave afterwards.

LATCH: play a chord and let go, and it keeps sounding. The next chord you play replaces it. Keys pressed while others are still down add to the chord. Pressing a key that's already sounding plays it again.

HOLD: every note you play joins the ones already sounding. When all the voices are in use, the oldest note stops to make room, so the new one always plays. Poly has six voices; mono, legato and unison have one. Press a held key again to stop just that note.

Keys you're holding when you turn latch on keep sounding when you let go, so you don't have to play them again. Switching between LATCH and HOLD while latch is on keeps the notes. Turning it off releases everything.

[[STOP]], a slot change and the drum key release the notes but leave LATCH or HOLD on. Latch is off after a restart, but your LATCH or HOLD choice stays. It only holds what you play on the keys: notes from a sequencer or from MIDI in are left alone, so latch stays out of the way while a sequencer runs.
