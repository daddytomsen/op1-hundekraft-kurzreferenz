STEFAN

A second tape machine: four heads, each with its own speed, reverse and loop.

STEFAN is op1r's free tape machine, by xoza. It plays the same four tracks as the OP-1's own tape, but every track gets a playhead of its own. Each head has its own speed, direction, loop and mute, so the four tracks can drift against each other. The OP-1's own tape keeps running underneath: the transport, the markers and your clips are the same ones.

STEFAN starts off. Turn it on with white on the first page of the settings. It takes 1 or 2 MB of the OP-1's memory while it's on and nothing when it's off. Once you've switched it on, it comes back on at the next start if there's memory for it. If there isn't, a message says so and it stays off.

While STEFAN is on, it takes over the tape screen:

  • The top left shows the active track's number and whether it plays, is stopped or records. The top shows the position of the active head, and the top right its speed, with REV when it runs backwards.
  • The middle shows eight seconds either side of that head: clips, its loop, bar marks and your markers.
  • The bottom shows all four tracks around their own heads, and the right edge shows the record level and the input meter.

[[T1]] to [[T4]]Pick the active track. With [[Shift]], mute or unmute it
[[Shift]] + [[STOP]] or [[PLAY]]Stop or start the active track on its own
[[STOP]] while stoppedThe active head goes to its loop start, or to 0 without a loop
Sound keys 1, 2 and 3Loop in, loop out and loop on or off, for the active head. [[Shift]] + key 3 loops the clip under the head
[[Shift]] + sound key 5Reverse
< and >Scrub the head. With [[Shift]], jump a bar
whiteSpeed, in steps from 1/4 to 4. Hold [[Shift]] to turn it finely
orangeRecord level
[[lift]], [[drop]] and [[scissors]]Work at the active head, with the OP-1's own clipboard. Undo sees them

REC records at the active track's head, at its speed and direction and inside its own loop, and the take becomes a clip on that track. The markers, undo and the tape edits work at the active head and its loop. Hold [[tape]] and press [[T1]] to [[T4]] to switch tapes as before; STEFAN's heads start again at the top of the new tape.

Note

STEFAN doesn't run in disk mode. A take plays about 3 to 4 dB louder through STEFAN than through the OP-1's own tape, as it does on op1r. With STEFAN on, the tracks on USB output come from STEFAN too.

STEFAN is from op1r by xoza (pcppcp). The kennel port is by hundekraft.
