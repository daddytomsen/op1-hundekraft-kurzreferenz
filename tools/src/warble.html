Warble

Chorus, vibrato and tape wobble, after Maiko.

A short delay whose time wobbles in two uneven rhythms, so the movement never repeats exactly. Half mix gives chorus; full mix gives vibrato. A slow rate and small depth give tape-like pitch movement. The cassette artwork comes from Maiko's #250 with permission; its hubs turn and the loose tape moves with the controls.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick warble on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Rate00–99

Slow to fast wobble

[[cap:knob-g]]
Depth00–99

Shallow to wide delay modulation; rises more gently in the lower half

[[cap:knob-w]]
Tone00–99

Dark to bright delayed signal

[[cap:knob-o]]
MixDry, chorus near the middle, vibrato at full mix

Blend dry and delayed signal

The screen shows 00 to 99 for each knob. There is one page. Press [[T3]] to bypass or enable it; bypass dims the screen and shows FX OFF. On a synth or the drums it is mono. On the master the left and right sides wobble a quarter-cycle apart, which makes the sound wider.
