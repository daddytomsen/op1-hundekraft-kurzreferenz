Pond

Pond is an LFO of its own. In the LFO list ([[Shift]] + [[T4]]), turn blue to POND instead of QUAD and press [[T4]]. Four species share a pond and compete for room. When one thrives the others shrink, and the balance keeps shifting, so four targets move together but never in lockstep. The screen plots species 1 against 3 across and 2 against 4 up, with a bar for each species' number.

Push an encoder to pick a species. green sets the chaos, from calm, regular swings up to wild. white sets that species' amount and orange its target. [[Shift]] + blue switches between free and synced speed, and [[Shift]] + green between free running and a restart at every note.
