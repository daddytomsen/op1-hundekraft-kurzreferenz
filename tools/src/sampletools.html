Sampler tools

Three tools on the stock sampler screens, for a synth sampler sound and a sampled drum kit. They're there whenever the stock sampler screen shows; while op1r's sample editor is open, they step aside.

Zoom. Push white to zoom in around the marker you moved last: 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x, up to 32x. [[Shift]] + push white zooms out again. Zooming doesn't move start or end. On the synth sampler, hold blue pushed and turn it to move the start in fine steps. Markers outside the view show as arrows at its edges. The zoom resets when you change the sound, the slot or the drum key.

Note and tuning (synth sampler). The screen shows the note of the part you use, in the OP-1's own numbering (middle C is C3), with a tuner from -50 to +50 cents. The needle turns green within 3 cents. Push to tune it automatically, or turn to tune it yourself, and then C plays C. [[Shift]] + push green tunes the sound to what it measured: every key plays in tune, the needle moves to the middle and the note turns green. Push again to put the old tuning back. To tune by hand, hold [[Shift]] and turn green for semitones or white for cents. Clockwise raises the sound, and the screen shows how far you've moved it, like +7 st +10 c. Turning by hand ends the push-again undo: [[Shift]] + push green then tunes from the measured pitch again. The idea is BarneyBrown's.

Fit to bars. Stretch the sample to exactly 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars at the OP-1's tempo:

  1. Push orange. The fit panel opens.
  2. Turn blue to pick 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. Lengths the sample can't fit are skipped; the first one that doesn't fit shows FULL.
  3. Turn green to choose KEEP PITCH (stretch the time, pitch stays) or TAPE (change the speed, pitch follows).
  4. Push orange again to fit. Any other key closes the panel.

Keys and knobs wait while it fits. Fitting can't be undone with tape undo. The markers follow the new length. On a drum kit the whole used part, from the earliest start to the latest end, is fitted and every key's markers move with it.

Note

Fitting changes the sound you're playing, not the saved one: reload it the usual way to get the original back. It doesn't work while you sample or record, or on a sample shorter than 0.05 seconds.
