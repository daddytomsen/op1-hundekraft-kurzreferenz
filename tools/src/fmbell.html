FM Bell

FM bells and e-pianos.

Two-operator FM for bells, e-pianos and metal. The clapper swings with the envelope and the rings beside the bell follow the modulation. Turn blue to choose the frequency ratio. The dial follows your choice.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick fmbell

[[cap:knob-b]]
Ratio

Modulator ratio, 0.5 to 8 in half steps

[[cap:knob-g]]
Index

Modulation index (brightness)

[[cap:knob-w]]
Decay

How fast the index dies away. Low is a long ring, high is a short pluck

[[cap:knob-o]]
Feedback

Feedback on the modulator. Adds grit
