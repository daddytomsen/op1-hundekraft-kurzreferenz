sim

sim is a synthesized drum kit by LizardMan. Pick it as the drum type: press [[drum]], hold [[Shift]] and press [[T1]]. Every hit throws a solid into a glass box on the screen. Low sounds make big ones, long decays make them bounce more, and metal sounds turn sharp-edged.

It comes with four kits in the drum preset browser: sim default, sim range, sim ghost and sim metal. Each key has eight settings, on [[T1]] like a D-Box kit. Hold [[Shift]] for the second four.

EncoderTurn[[Shift]] + turn
bluePitch, 28 Hz to 7.2 kHzColour: a filter that goes from low-pass to band-pass to high-pass
greenDecay, 15 ms to 3.8 sShape: sine, soft square, square, then metal
whiteSweep: a pitch drop of up to 5 octavesChoke group 1 to 4, or off
orangeNoiseDrive: soft clip, then crunch

Turn a knob and the box shows that key's throw for about two seconds. SHOW VALUES on the OPUS PLUGINS settings page puts the numbers on screen too.
