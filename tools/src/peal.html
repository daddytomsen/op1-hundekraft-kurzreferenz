Peal

Peal plays English change ringing. Pick PEAL with [[Shift]] + [[seq]], then hold 3 to 8 keys: each is a bell, and the method changes their order on each row. One or two keys are silent. When you release the last key, the current row finishes and stops. Hold [[Shift]] while releasing it to keep ringing. Hold [[Shift]] and turn orange left to stop.

A call changes the order at a set point in the method. The handstroke gap adds a step of silence after every second row. A new chord changes the bells at the next row; changing the number of bells restarts their order. [[Shift]] + the high C# starts or stops using the last bell set; hold [[REC]] too to record it to tape from the start. Settings and bells are saved; playback comes back stopped.
