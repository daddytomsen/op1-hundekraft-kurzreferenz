Crush

Bit depth, sample rate and drive, after Maiko.

Crush reduces the signal's bit depth and sample rate, then adds drive. Low bit depth makes a waveform's steps audible; a low sample rate adds aliasing. The artwork is Maiko's, used with permission. It runs on synth, drum and master FX; the master keeps the two channels separate.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick crush on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Bits1–16 bits

Quantization depth

[[cap:knob-g]]
RateAbout 0.3 kHz up to full rate, shown in kHz

Sample rate: the lower it goes, the longer each sample is held

[[cap:knob-w]]
DriveAbout 0–24 dB

Gain into soft clipping

[[cap:knob-o]]
MixDry to fully wet

Blend dry and crushed signal

It starts at 6 bits, 8.0 kHz, 6 dB of drive and full wet. There is one page. Press [[T3]] to bypass or enable the effect.
