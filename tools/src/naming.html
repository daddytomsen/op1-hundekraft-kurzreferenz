Name your sounds

  1. Hold a sound key (1 to 8) to save as usual. When the OP-1 would save, a NAME screen opens instead.
  2. Turn blue (LETTER on the screen) to move one letter, green (ROW) to move one row.
  3. Push white (ADD) to add the letter. Pushing blue adds it too. Press [[left]] (DEL) to delete the last one.
  4. Push orange (OK, the red ring on the screen), or press the same sound key again, to save with that name.

Names are up to 12 characters: a to z, 0 to 9, dash, underscore and space. If the name exists, the new sound gets _1 added. Leave it empty, or press a mode key, to get the usual date-stamp name. It works for synth and drum sounds. The NAME screen starts with the name of the preset the sound came from, so a second save of the same sound is one push of orange.

Overwrite, rename and delete

With Naming in your build you can change your own presets, synth sounds and drum kits alike. Factory presets can't be changed.

  • Overwrite: hold [[synth]] ([[drum]] for a kit) and the sound key while it saves. The save clock says OVERWRITING, and the sound replaces the preset it came from, under the same name. A sound that came from a factory preset is saved as a new one.
  • Rename: open the preset browser ([[Shift]] + a sound key), select one of your presets and push white. The RENAME screen opens with its name; change it and push orange.
  • Delete: in the browser, hold [[Shift]] and hold white down. The save clock counts down DELETING, and the preset is gone when it runs out. Let go of white or [[Shift]] to cancel.

A deleted preset is kept in a .deleted folder next to your presets, the newest 16 of them, so it can be brought back by hand in disk mode.
