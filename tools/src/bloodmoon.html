Bloodmoon

A dark organ with a haunted bell.

Bloodmoon by Nola Drift (SammiBabe666) is a gothic organ. Every note is a pipe with its 2nd, 3rd and 4th harmonics, and a bell strikes on top of it. The bell's two partials sit a little off the organ, close to a minor third, so it never quite agrees with the pipes. A slow vibrato fades in after the attack, with a faint wobble under it, so held notes drift like old pipes.

Load[[synth]] then [[Shift]] + [[T1]] pick bloodmoon

The screen is a cathedral under a blood moon. The moon rises with orange, the rose window turns with blue, the tower bells swing with green and the stars flicker with white. Each encoder's name and value are beside it.

[[cap:knob-b]]
Blood

How rich the organ is. Thin and hollow at 0, thick and dark at the top

[[cap:knob-g]]
Eclipse

The bell: how hard it strikes and how long it rings. No bell at 0, a loud, long, out-of-tune bell at the top

[[cap:knob-w]]
Haunting

Vibrato depth, up to about three quarters of a semitone either way, plus a steady pitch wobble

[[cap:knob-o]]
Moonrise

Vibrato speed, from a slow 0.15 Hz to 9 Hz. Most of the travel is at the slow end

Shape the attack and release on [[T2]]. There are twelve Bloodmoon presets: cathedral (the default), funeral bell, crypt drone, witch choir, hollow saint, moon tremor, sunken pipes, blood rite, banshee, dusk swell, grave bass and organ stab.

It's from Project Bloodmoon by Nola Drift, published by SammiBabe666. Kennel port by hundekraft.
