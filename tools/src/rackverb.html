rackverb

The late-80s studio rack, in one effect.

rackverb is a rack of four effects units from the late 80s, with 316 programs between them: big halls, gated drum rooms and reverse reverbs, then flangers, slapback echoes and wide choruses. VERB I has 63 programs: reverbs, gated rooms and reverse rooms. VERB II has 100, from 00 to 99: reverbs, gates, reverses, flangers, choruses, delays and a few odd ones. Program 00 is a bypass. FEX has 63 echoes, multi-tap delays, slapbacks and stereo wideners. QUAD is a multi-effect with 90 programs built from EQ, chorus, flanger, phaser, a rotating speaker, delay and reverb. Pick a unit, turn orange one click per program, and read the program's name under the number.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick rackverb on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Feedback

Sends the effect back into itself for longer tails and more repeats. Each program stops short of running away

Push: Size: shrinks every delay in the program, down to half

[[cap:knob-g]]
Cutoff

Darkens the effect, from 11 kHz down to 500 Hz

Push: Mode: AUTH or CLEAN

[[cap:knob-w]]
Mix

Dry to fully wet. At zero you hear only the dry sound

Push: Unit: VERB I, VERB II, FEX or QUAD

[[cap:knob-o]]
Program

One program per click. It wraps round: after the unit's last program comes the first, and back from the first you land on the last

AUTH gives the grainy, low-resolution sound of the old units. CLEAN keeps the same programs with less noise and a little more top end. When you change program, unit or mode, the effect fades out for a moment and comes back with the new program, while the dry sound keeps playing.

It starts on VERB I, program 14, with Feedback at 0, Cutoff at 10 kHz and Mix at 50. Press [[T3]] to bypass or enable it. rackverb is mono on synth and drum. As a master effect it takes the mix in mono and plays back in stereo. It is one of the heavier effects, so if a busy synth crackles, check the Resource monitor.

The programs are our own, written for this effect in the style of each unit's program list. rackverb contains no code or data from the original units.
