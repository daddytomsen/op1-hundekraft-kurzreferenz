In a room

Every pass of the loop goes through a room.

After Alvin Lucier's "I am sitting in a room" (1969). You record a phrase, and on every pass the loop plays through a model of a room and is recorded again. The room's resonances pile up, and after enough passes speech melts into a chord. The counter shows the generation. Choose one of four rooms with Shape. Push blue to set Loop to a free length or one of six tempo-synced lengths.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick in a room on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Size

Longest wall, 0.8 m to 8 m

Push: Loop: free 0.5 s to 7.6 s, or synced 1/16 to 2 bars

[[cap:knob-g]]
Shape

CUBE, GOLDEN, BOLT or LUCIER

Push: Absorb: treble lost on each pass

[[cap:knob-w]]
Erode

How hard each pass is filtered

Push: Crossfade: 0–93 ms across a captured loop boundary

[[cap:knob-o]]
Mix

Mix, dry to the loop

Push: record (free) or arm (synced)

Crossfade smooths the join where the loop ends and starts again. Set it before you record; zero leaves the join as it is. Free loops longer than 3.8 s and long synced loops, such as two bars at a fast tempo, are recorded at lower quality, and the LOOP line says LO-FI. If a synced length doesn't fit even then, it's halved, and the LOOP line says what you'll get, for example SYNC 1 BAR AS 1/2. The screen shows ARMED while it waits for the grid.

If you slow the tape or the tempo while a synced loop plays, the loop restarts on a shorter part of itself, on the beat, instead of going quiet until the next bar. Back at full speed it plays whole again.

  1. Put In a room on the master effect for best results.
  2. Push blue to switch it to LOOP and set the loop length first. It's read when a capture starts. Push blue again to go back.
  3. Push orange to capture. In synced mode the capture waits for the next matching grid point and lasts exactly the loop length.
  4. Listen to the generations stack up. Push orange again to capture something new.
Tip

Small CUBE and GOLDEN rooms give clear chords. The LUCIER shape is a guess at the room Lucier recorded in; large rooms hum very low and you may not hear it on the built-in speaker. Before the first capture the wet signal is silent.
