Tape edits

Four edits for building a song while it plays. All of them work on the tape screen, also while the tape plays, but not while it records.

KeysEditWhat happens
[[Shift]] + [[drop]]MergeMixes the lifted tracks together and drops the result onto the selected track at the playhead, replacing what's there. With one lifted track it's a plain drop. It takes the place of the stock [[Shift]] + [[drop]]
Hold [[tape]] + [[lift]]Copy the loopCopies all four tracks inside the loop without cutting anything. Needs the loop on and shorter than about 22 seconds
Hold [[tape]] + [[drop]]Paste after the loopPastes the copy right after the loop and moves the loop onto it, so the next paste extends the song. With the loop off, it pastes after the last clip. A copy goes onto all four tracks, one lifted track onto the selected one
Hold [[tape]] + [[Shift]] + [[drop]]BounceMixes all four tracks inside the loop onto the selected track. The other tracks stay as they are. Needs the loop on

A popup names the edit (MERGE, COPY, PASTE or BOUNCE) and shows the progress. Merge, paste and bounce are one undo step each; copy changes nothing on the tape, so it isn't a step. Undoing a paste takes the audio back out, but the loop stays where it moved.

Mixes are added up exactly, without the mixer's volume or pan. If the result would clip, the whole edit goes through a soft limiter.

Tip

To build a song live: set a loop around four bars, hold [[tape]] and press [[lift]] to copy it, then hold [[tape]] and press [[drop]]. The loop jumps to the copy. Record something new on top, and paste again.

When an edit can't run, a short message says why, such as "bounce needs the loop on", "copy needs the loop on", "loop too long to copy", "the clipboard is empty" or "no room left on the tape".
