STUTTER

A beat-locked stutter.

STUTTER cuts time into slices on the OP-1's beat. At each slice it rolls a dice: the slice plays through, or its first part is repeated until the slice ends. Repeats always land on the grid. It follows the OP-1's tempo, MIDI clock and the tape's speed, and runs at 120 BPM when there's no clock. The four rings show Rate, Mode, Shape and Chance. Push blue and the Rate ring shows Parts; push again to return to Rate.

Load[[Shift]] + [[T3]] pick stutter on a synth or drum slot

[[cap:knob-b]]
Rate

Slice length: 1/4, 1/8, 1/8 triplet, 1/16, 1/16 triplet, 1/32

Push: Parts: how many repeats a stuttered slice is cut into, 2 to 16

[[cap:knob-g]]
Mode

REPEAT, BRAKE (each repeat slower and lower, like a tape stop), REVERSE, or GATE (chops the live sound instead)

[[cap:knob-w]]
Shape

Fade on each repeat, 2 to 12 ms. In GATE: how long each burst stays open

[[cap:knob-o]]
Chance

How many slices stutter, from none to all

Push: freeze

Push orange to freeze: STUTTER keeps repeating the part that plays now, across slices, until you push again. Freeze isn't saved with the sound.

Note

On the screen the four rings sit in the corners: Rate top left, Mode top right, Shape bottom left, Chance bottom right. A slice can't be longer than about 1.5 seconds, so 1/4 at a very slow tempo is cut short.
